Why Guangdong

Our base is the point.
Guangdong is the advantage.

We consolidate inside the Pearl River Delta — the world's densest belt of furniture, decor and home-goods manufacturing. That location is part of the service: shorter trips to the factories, lower port charges, and one coordinated supply chain from the factory floor to the container.

01

Foshan furniture cluster

Foshan — Shunde, Lecong and Longjiang — is one of the largest furniture manufacturing and wholesale hubs on earth. We are on its doorstep: faster sampling, easy factory audits and lower inland freight before goods ever reach the port.

02

Ports at our doorstep

Guangzhou, Shenzhen and the Foshan river ports all sit close to our warehouse. Shorter drayage means lower port-handling charges (到港费) and multiple weekly sailings to Australia, the US, Europe and the Middle East.

03

One coordinated chain

Raw materials, hardware, upholstery, finishing, export packaging and carton printing all sit within the Greater Bay Area. One partner, one warehouse, one set of documents — not a patchwork of middlemen.

04

A mature export ecosystem

Decades of export know-how surround us — experienced freight forwarders, customs brokers, container lines and both LCL and FCL consolidation, with export documentation handled for smooth global shipping.

What we source most often

The product series
our buyers ask for.

Decor orders almost always mix together. Most buyers send a list that touches three or four categories at once — which is exactly why consolidation matters, and why we keep one warehouse for the whole order.

01

Wall decor & mirrors

Framed art, mirrors, wall panels, metal wall decor and decorative clocks — including oversized pieces that need careful crating.

02

Storage & organization

Woven, fabric or board-struct baskets, boxes and trays, plus shelving accessories, drawer organizers and wardrobe storage.

03

Textiles & soft furnishings

Cushions, throws, curtains, table linens, rugs and table runners — matched to a colour or fabric reference you send.

04

Decorative accessories

Vases, bowls, trays, candle holders, sculptures and table decor in ceramic, metal and resin. We skip fragile glass — it is the hardest to consolidate and ship safely.

05

Furniture & interiors

From single pieces to full project fit-outs. Our engineering experience covers hotels, residences and commercial spaces: we read drawings and BOMs, coordinate custom manufacturing, and handle FF&E for complete projects.

06

Candles & home fragrance

Scented candles, diffusers, reed sets and wax melts, with scent, vessel and packaging options to match your brand.

07

Artificial plants & planters

Faux greenery, dried-flower arrangements and planters in ceramic, fibre-clay, metal and rattan.
