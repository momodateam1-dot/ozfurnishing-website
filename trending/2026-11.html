Signal 01

Where the momentum is

Relative heat by platform, normalised to 100 at the strongest marketplace. Percentages are year-on-year movement reported in public category summaries.

  • TikTok Shop US No fresh year-on-year disclosure, so the Q3 figure is carried forward; EchoTik puts Q3 across 15 markets at about $32B GMV, up 3% quarter on quarter, with the US at $7.5-8B. Beauty still dominates, but home bedding and power stations took multiple top-ten slots in late October.
  • Amazon US Carried-forward Q3 growth rate; no new YoY disclosure. Prime Big Deal Days on Oct 6-7 pulled kitchen and storage demand forward, FBA fulfilment fees rose on Oct 15, and Black Friday inbound cutoffs closed mid-month.
  • Temu US / EU The Q2 growth rate is carried forward, but October itself was rough: US semi-managed sellers reported traffic falling to less than half of September from mid-month, EU sellers reported drops above 30%, and the platform published no explanation. Q3 semi-managed GMV share reached 47%.
  • AliExpress Global No new growth disclosure, so the previous figure is carried forward. October trend lists (Copyfy, Accio, AliShopping Tools) all point the same way: winter-comfort goods, kitchen tools and home lighting are the categories rising before Double 11 and Black Friday.

  • TikTok Shop UK / EU The UK growth rate is carried forward, but October weekly charts confirm the structure: a home-goods chart, not a beauty chart. Treadmills, anti-slip rugs, Shark heating and cooling, cordless vacuums, Christmas gift boxes and, in the week of Oct 26-Nov 1, BLUETTI at number one.

How to read this

Method, and its limits

Rankings are compiled from the October 2026 public record: Kalodata TikTok Shop 24-market weekly product charts (weeks of Oct 19-25 and Oct 26-Nov 1), TikTok Shop US weekly charts for Oct 12-18 as republished by cross-border trade press, EchoTik's Q3 tally (15 markets, roughly $32B GMV, up 3% quarter on quarter, with the US at $7.5-8B), Amazon Prime Big Deal Days deal reporting for Oct 6-7 plus best-seller badges observed mid-October, Temu's Q3 semi-managed disclosure alongside seller-reported October traffic data, Copyfy's October dropshipping list built on AliExpress real-time sales, and the Accio (Oct 11) and AliShopping Tools (Oct 9) trend analyses. Unit and revenue figures are the most recent published values, not live API pulls. Platform percentages are year-on-year where published and quarter-on-quarter or month-on-month where that is all that was reported; Amazon, Shein and TikTok Shop UK/EU move on quarterly disclosure cycles and may lag the reporting month. Where a platform published nothing new this month, the previous edition's figure is carried forward and flagged on the card rather than replaced with a guess. Category shares, retail price bands and every sourcing range are OZ estimates derived from those same public reports and are marked as such.

We publish this because most sourcing advice is written after the trend has already peaked. This list is deliberately short. If a product is here it means the sales data is public and the supplier landscape around it is real — not that it will still be moving in six months.

Two things we will not do: quote a factory price we have not checked, or recommend a product we cannot sample. Use this page as a starting conversation, not a purchase order.
