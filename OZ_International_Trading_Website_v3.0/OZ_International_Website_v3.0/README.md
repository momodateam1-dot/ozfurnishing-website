# OZ International Website v3.0

## Positioning
Premium China trading & sourcing partner.
Core conversion: product inquiry / procurement inquiry.
No direct online checkout. All product CTAs lead to inquiry.html.

## Six-page structure
1. Home — brand credibility, capabilities and two customer routes
2. C-End Sourcing — small-value products / personal sourcing; no shopping cart
3. B2B Procurement — engineering, project, wholesale and bulk procurement
4. Process — inquiry to sourcing to confirmation to delivery support
5. About — company positioning and strengths
6. Inquiry — strong lead form + multiple contact channels + FAQ

## Important
Contact details are demo placeholders and must be replaced before launch.
The product visuals are CSS-generated editorial placeholders in this prototype; replace with licensed product photography before production.
