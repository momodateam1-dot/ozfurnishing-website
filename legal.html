Nature of this website

This website is an information and enquiry channel for OZ International, a China-based sourcing and trade coordination desk. It describes services, outlines how we work, and gives buyers a way to start a conversation. It is not an offer in the contractual sense, and nothing on it becomes binding until it is confirmed in writing by both parties.

No online commerce

This site deliberately provides no shopping cart, no checkout, no instant freight calculation and no automated shipment tracking. Product and category pages route visitors into an enquiry conversation, because every sourcing request is quoted against real supplier capacity, specification and destination. Prices shown anywhere on this site, if any, are illustrative only.

Indicative information only

Specifications, materials, dimensions, lead times, minimum order quantities, certifications and indicative price ranges are provided as a starting point for discussion. They are subject to supplier confirmation, sample approval, order quantity, specification changes and market conditions. The only figures you should rely on are those in a written quotation or pro forma invoice we issue to you.

Regulatory and certification requirements differ by destination market. Confirming that a product meets the rules of your country remains the buyer's responsibility; we support that process but do not certify compliance on your behalf.
