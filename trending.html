How to read this

Method, and its limits

Rankings are compiled from publicly reported marketplace data: TikTok Shop US category GMV and creator-commerce reports, Amazon category best-seller rank movements, Temu editorial best-seller roundups, and AliExpress order-velocity feeds. Unit and revenue figures are the most recent published values, not live API pulls. Price bands and category shares are OZ estimates derived from those same public reports and are marked as such.

We publish this because most sourcing advice is written after the trend has already peaked. This list is deliberately short. If a product is here it means the sales data is public and the supplier landscape around it is real — not that it will still be moving in six months.

Two things we will not do: quote a factory price we have not checked, or recommend a product we cannot sample. Use this page as a starting conversation, not a purchase order.
