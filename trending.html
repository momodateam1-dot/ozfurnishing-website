How to read this

Method, and its limits

Rankings are compiled from the September 2026 public record: TikTok Shop US and UK weekly product charts as republished by EchoTik, FastMoss and cross-border trade press, Amazon US best-seller rank and search-velocity reporting, Temu best-seller roundups plus Temu's own Q2 GMV disclosure, and AliExpress order-velocity leaderboards. Unit and revenue figures are the most recent published values, not live API pulls. Platform percentages are year-on-year where published and quarter-on-quarter or month-on-month where that is all that was reported; Amazon, Shein, AliExpress and TikTok Shop UK/EU move on quarterly disclosure cycles and may lag the reporting month. Where a platform published nothing new this month, the previous edition's figure is carried forward and flagged on the card rather than replaced with a guess. Category shares, retail price bands and every sourcing range are OZ estimates derived from those same public reports and are marked as such.

We publish this because most sourcing advice is written after the trend has already peaked. This list is deliberately short. If a product is here it means the sales data is public and the supplier landscape around it is real — not that it will still be moving in six months.

Two things we will not do: quote a factory price we have not checked, or recommend a product we cannot sample. Use this page as a starting conversation, not a purchase order.
