The B2B buying flow

Seven stages.
You approve each one.

Nothing moves to the next stage without your sign-off. You always know which factories are being considered, what the money is for, and when the goods will be ready.

  1. 01

    Send the list or brief

    Send your BOQ, drawings, spec sheet or reference links together with target prices, quantities, deadlines and the destination port.

    Day 1
    You send

    BOQ, CAD or PDF drawings, target prices and quantities.

    We do

    Review feasibility, flag gaps and confirm the real scope.

    You get

    A clarified requirement list we both work from.

  2. 02

    We shortlist and verify factories

    We approach suitable factories, check business licences and export rights, judge real capacity, and compare them against your specification.

    2 – 4 days
    You send

    Any preferred factory, or a list to avoid.

    We do

    Approach factories, verify licences and audit real capacity.

    You get

    A shortlist with honest capability notes, including weaknesses.

  3. 03

    Quotes, terms and trade language

    We bring every quote onto the same Incoterm, MOQ, packaging and lead-time basis so the numbers can be compared line by line.

    3 – 7 days
    You send

    Preferred Incoterm, packaging and payment structure.

    We do

    Normalise each quote so nothing hides in the small print.

    You get

    An apples-to-apples comparison table you can decide from.

  4. 04

    Samples, materials and approval

    We arrange samples or material swatches, courier them to you, collect feedback and keep a sealed approved sample as the production reference.

    7 – 20 days
    You send

    What you need to approve — finish, colour, size, feel.

    We do

    Produce or source samples and courier them to you.

    You get

    A sealed approved sample that production must match.

  5. 05

    Contract, deposit and schedule

    Once terms are agreed we issue a proforma invoice, confirm payment structure, and lock a dated production schedule with clear milestones.

    3 – 5 days
    You send

    Signed proforma invoice and the deposit.

    We do

    Secure the production slot and confirm each milestone date.

    You get

    A dated schedule you can plan your project around.

  6. 06

    Production follow-up and inspection

    We track progress on the line, run a mid-production check, and carry out a pre-shipment inspection against AQL before the balance is released.

    Production cycle
    You send

    Your required AQL level or inspection standard.

    We do

    Check progress on site and inspect finished goods with photos.

    You get

    An inspection report before you release the balance payment.

  7. 07

    Consolidation, loading and export

    Goods from several factories are brought together, loaded under supervision, cleared for export, and handed over with a complete document set.

    3 – 7 days
    You send

    Forwarder details, or ask us to arrange the booking.

    We do

    Consolidate, supervise loading and clear China export.

    You get

    Full document set and tracking through to the vessel.

Factory direct vs. buying office

What changes
when we sit in between.

Many buyers can already reach a factory. The difference is what happens after the first quotation.

On your own

Factory direct

  • One factory's quote, with no benchmark to compare against
  • Licence, capacity and export rights taken on trust
  • Specification gaps discovered during production
  • Progress updates only when you ask for them
  • Balance usually requested before anything is inspected
  • Multiple factories mean multiple shipments and document sets
Through OZ

Buying office

  • Three or more factories quoted on identical terms
  • Licences, capacity and export rights checked before quoting
  • Specification gaps flagged before the deposit is paid
  • Scheduled progress updates through the whole cycle
  • Balance released only after a photo-backed AQL inspection
  • Everything consolidated into one container and one file
Common questions

Before you send
the first BOQ.

These are the questions professional buyers ask us first.

What is the minimum order quantity for B2B orders?

It depends on the product. MOQs are set by the factory, not by us, and usually range from one carton for stocked items to a few hundred pieces for custom production. We confirm the real MOQ with each factory before you commit to anything.

How do you verify a factory is legitimate?

We check the business licence and registered scope, confirm export rights, review certifications relevant to your market, and visit or video-audit the production line. We tell you what we found, including the weaknesses, before you choose a supplier.

Can you work with suppliers we already use?

Yes. Many buyers come to us with an existing supplier list and ask us to manage communication, quality and consolidation instead. We are also happy to quote the same items against alternative factories so you can see whether your current pricing still holds.

What payment terms do you support?

The common structure is a 30 percent deposit with the balance released after the pre-shipment inspection passes. We also work with letters of credit at sight, and for long-standing clients we can discuss alternative structures per project.

Will you sign an NDA before we share drawings?

Yes. We are used to working under NDA for hotel FF&E schedules, retail fit-out drawings and private-label product designs. Send your template with the brief, or ask us for ours, and we will return it signed before any file is opened.

Can you consolidate goods from several factories into one container?

Yes. This is one of the main reasons buyers use us. We collect from each factory, hold everything in one consolidation warehouse, check quantities against your BOQ, load under supervision and ship as a single consignment with one document set.

What inspection standard do you use?

Standard practice is an AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspection on general merchandise, with AQL 4.0 accepted on some categories and tighter levels available where your market requires it. We send a photo report before you release the balance payment.

Do you handle export paperwork and China customs clearance?

Yes. We prepare the commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and any fumigation or phytosanitary documents needed for wooden items, and we handle export declaration in China. Import clearance at your end stays with your broker.
