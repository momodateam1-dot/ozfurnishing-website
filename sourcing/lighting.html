Brass, steel, aluminium and stainless, in plated, brushed, powder-coated or hand-patinated finishes. Ask for coating thickness on anything going into a bathroom or coastal property.

Common questions

Before you send
the first enquiry.

These are the questions that decide whether a lighting order can be installed without surprises.

What certification do your lights carry?

It depends on what your market requires and what the factory holds. Common marks are CE and UKCA for Europe and the UK, UL or ETL for North America, and SAA for Australia, alongside RoHS. Tell us the installation country and we will confirm which marks the fixture can actually be supplied with, rather than quoting a generic claim.

Can you supply the right voltage for my country?

Yes. Voltage is specified at the time of order, typically 110–120V at 60Hz for North America and 220–240V at 50Hz elsewhere. Integrated LED fixtures may also need a driver rated for your supply, which we confirm with the factory before production rather than after it.

Are the lamps dimmable?

Only if specified that way. Dimmability depends on both the driver and the dimmer type in your building, so we ask which dimming protocol is already installed before quoting. Retrofitting a driver after installation costs far more than choosing the right one up front.

Can you make a fixture from a drawing?

Yes. Architectural and custom decorative fixtures are regularly built to a drawing or a reference photograph, with a sample made first for approval. Bespoke work generally starts at twenty to fifty units and takes longer than a stocked item, so it suits projects with a fixed schedule.
