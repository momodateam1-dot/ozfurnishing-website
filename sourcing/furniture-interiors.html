Catalogues are huge, photographs are reused between factories, and the difference between a good batch and a bad one is usually in the frame, the foam and the finish — none of which show up in a listing. We buy furniture out of the Foshan and Shunde belt regularly, and we inspect it before it leaves.

Materials and construction

What it is made of
and what to ask for.

Most furniture disputes come down to a material that was described loosely. Naming the material, the finish and the standard up front is the cheapest quality control you can buy.

01

Solid wood

Oak, ash, walnut, rubberwood and beech. Ask for kiln-dried stock at 8–12% moisture content — anything wetter moves and cracks after it reaches a drier climate.

↗
02

Engineered board

Plywood, MDF and particle board with veneer, melamine or lacquer. Cheaper and more dimensionally stable than solid wood; specify the formaldehyde class (E0, CARB or EPA) if your market requires it.

↗
03

Metal

Steel, stainless steel and aluminium, powder-coated, brushed or plated. Check coating thickness and whether the frame is welded or bolted — it changes both strength and flat-pack feasibility.

↗
04

Rattan, cane & woven

Natural rattan and cane webbing, plus PE rattan woven over a frame. Natural material needs consistent grading, or colour drift shows across a container.

↗
05

Upholstery

Polyester, linen blends and velvet fabrics, PU, and top-grain or split leather. Specify foam density, Martindale rub count and any fire-retardancy treatment your market requires.

↗
06

Stone & sintered surfaces

Marble, granite, sintered stone and tempered glass for table tops and consoles. Heavy, fragile and usually worth crating rather than cartoning.

Before you send the enquiry

Six things worth
deciding before you ask.

The more of this you can send in the first message, the fewer rounds it takes to get to a number you can actually use.

Room dimensions, door widths and stair access — oversized sofas and beds fail at delivery more often than at the factory
Intended use: residential, rental, hospitality or commercial, because it changes foam density, frame joints and fabric rub count
Finish and colour reference — a Pantone, RAL code or physical swatch beats a photograph every time
Fabric or leather specification, including Martindale rub count and any fire-retardancy requirement
Packaging preference: flat-pack for freight efficiency, or assembled and crated for immediate use
Target landed cost per piece, so we can quote to a number instead of guessing what you can absorb

Send whatever you have — a screenshot, a dimensions sketch or a half-finished list. Missing pieces we can fill in together; wrong assumptions are what cost time.

Common questions

Before you send
the first enquiry.

These are the questions that come up on almost every furniture brief.

What is the minimum order for custom furniture?

It is set by the factory, not by us. Stocked designs can often be bought one piece at a time, while a custom size, finish or fabric usually needs ten to fifty units per model because the factory has to set up a production run. We confirm the real number with each factory before you commit.

Can you reproduce a design I saw somewhere else?

Usually yes, if you can send photographs, dimensions and any detail shots of the joints and finish. We will tell you honestly where a design can be matched closely and where the original uses a process or material that is not worth reproducing at your volume.

How much does it cost to ship furniture from China?

Furniture is bulky, so freight is charged on volume rather than weight for most sea shipments. A consolidated part-container load is far cheaper per piece than a small standalone shipment. We quote freight on the actual packed volume after consolidation, not on an estimate made before the goods are packed.

Do you handle hotel and project furniture?

Yes. Hotel, villa and restaurant furniture is bought against a BOQ and a handover date rather than a single product link, and it goes through our B2B buying flow with factory shortlisting, sampling and pre-shipment inspection included.
