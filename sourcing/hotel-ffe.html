Materials and construction

What it is specified in
and why it matters.

Contract specification exists because hotel furniture is used ten times harder than domestic furniture and is regulated more tightly. These are the lines that get written into a BOQ.

01

Contract-grade frames

Kiln-dried hardwood or reinforced engineered frames with screwed and glued joints rather than staples. Built for a commercial warranty, not a living room.

↗
02

Commercial upholstery

Contract fabrics rated at 40,000 Martindale rubs or more, with foam density specified for the seat, not just the look.

↗
03

Fire-retardant treatment

BS 5852 Crib 5 or CAL 117 depending on your market, applied to foam, fabric and filling. Ask for the certificate by item, not by supplier.

↗
04

Casegoods surfaces

High-pressure laminate, veneer or lacquer on engineered board, specified for wear, cleanability and replacement cycles.

↗
05

Metal & stone

Powder-coated steel and aluminium, stainless, marble and sintered stone for reception desks and high-traffic public areas.

↗
06

Bedding & towelling

Thread count, GSM and fibre content specified per item, with commercial laundering durability in mind rather than retail softness.

Before you send the enquiry

Six things worth
deciding before you ask.

A BOQ is enough to start. These six answers turn it into a schedule we can hold a factory to.

Room types and counts, with the item list per room type
Handover date, and whether installation is phased by floor or by wing
Fire-retardancy standard for your market, stated per item category
Which items are bespoke to a drawing and which can be bought from stock
Whether a mock-up room or material sample approval is required before bulk
Consolidation and shipping plan: one container, part load, or staged deliveries

Send the BOQ even if it is unfinished. We will tell you which lines are straightforward and which will drive the schedule.

Factory-by-factory vs. one buying office

when one party owns the schedule.

A fit-out involves more factories than any buyer wants to manage individually. The risk is not price, it is timing and mismatch.

Common questions

Before you send
the first BOQ.

These are the questions project buyers ask before handing over drawings.

Can you pack and label by room?

Yes, and on a fit-out it is worth insisting on. We pack by room type and label by room and floor, so the installation crew unpacks in sequence instead of sorting a container on site. It costs a little more in the warehouse and saves considerably more in labour and delay at the other end.

How long does a hotel fit-out take to procure?

Sixty to one hundred and twenty days from approved drawings to container loading is typical, depending on how much is bespoke. Custom casegoods and upholstery drive the schedule; stocked items are much faster. We give you dated milestones at the start and flag any factory slippage as soon as it appears, not at the end.

Can you build a mock-up room first?

Yes, and we recommend it for anything bespoke. A mock-up room, or a full set of material and finish samples if a room is impractical, settles comfort, colour and build quality before a container is committed. It is the cheapest insurance available on a project of this size.

Will you work from our designer's drawings and BOQ?

Yes. We work from the designer's drawings, BOQ, FF&E schedule or reference images, and we will tell you where a specified item is difficult to source or better quoted elsewhere. We are also used to working under NDA, and will sign before any drawing is opened.
