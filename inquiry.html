FAQ / Common questions

A few things buyers
often ask first.

Every request is different. These quick answers explain how to start and what to expect from the first conversation.

Can I purchase directly through the website?

No. OZ is a sourcing and trade inquiry website, not a direct-checkout store. Send the product or category you are interested in and our team will confirm details, availability, quotation and next steps.

Can you source a product from a photo or link?

Yes. Share a reference photo, product link, screenshot or description. Add any important details such as dimensions, material, target budget, quantity and destination country.

Do you handle both small orders and bulk procurement?

We accept inquiries for small-value consumer products as well as B2B purchasing, project procurement and larger-volume requirements. Feasibility, minimum order and scope depend on the specific item and supplier.

Can you support hotel, villa or commercial projects?

You can send the project background, product schedule, BOQ, drawings or reference list. We will review the requirement and discuss what sourcing or procurement coordination may be suitable.

Can you arrange shipping or combine products from multiple suppliers?

Delivery coordination or consolidation can be discussed where it fits the purchase and is agreed in advance. We do not offer an instant shipping calculator or automated tracking platform on this website.

How soon will I receive a quotation?

Timing depends on how complete the requirement is and whether supplier confirmation is needed. Including product specifications, quantity and destination from the start helps us review it more efficiently.

Do I need a company to submit an inquiry?

No. Individuals can ask about small-value product sourcing, while companies and project buyers can submit bulk, commercial or engineering procurement requests.
